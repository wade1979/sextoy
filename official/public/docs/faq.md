# 5. Troubleshooting
Connectivity, app issues, firmware, etc.

## 5.1 The app can’t find my device
Try:
1. Ensure Bluetooth is on  
2. Restart the device  
3. Restart the app  
4. Move closer than 1 meter  

---

## 5.2 Motion feels inconsistent or delayed
May occur if:
- Network is unstable  
- AI mode is generating new patterns  
- Battery is low  

Check connection and try Manual Mode for calibration.

---

## 5.3 Firmware update failed
Restart the device and app; the updater will retry automatically.
