Quick Start

Get your AI Pleasure device up and running in minutes. Follow these simple steps to start your journey.

First Power On

When you first receive your device:

  1. Remove the device from its packaging
  2. Check that all components are included (device, USB-C cable, charging adapter)
  3. Before first use, fully charge the device using the included USB-C cable
  4. Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds to turn on the device
  5. You should see the LED indicator light up, indicating the device is powered on

Note: The device may take up to 2 hours for the initial charge.

Charging Mechanism

Proper charging ensures optimal battery performance:

  • Charging Time: Approximately 2 hours for a full charge
  • Charging Indicator: LED will blink during charging and turn solid when fully charged
  • Battery Life: Up to 2 hours of continuous use on a full charge
  • Charging Port: Use the included USB-C cable and any standard USB power adapter
  • Best Practice: Charge the device when the LED indicator turns red (low battery)

Important: Always ensure the charging port is dry before connecting the charger. Do not use the device while charging.

WiFi Configuration

Connecting your device to WiFi enables AI features and OTA updates:

  1. Power on the device and wait for it to enter setup mode (indicated by blinking WiFi LED)
  2. On your smartphone, go to WiFi settings and connect to the network named "AI-Pleasure-XXXX"
  3. Open your mobile browser and navigate to 192.168.4.1 or the setup page will open automatically
  4. Select your home WiFi network from the list and enter your WiFi password
  5. Wait for the device to connect (WiFi LED will turn solid when connected)
  6. Download and open the AI Pleasure mobile app
  7. The app will automatically detect and pair with your device

Troubleshooting: If connection fails, ensure your router supports 2.4GHz WiFi (required), and try moving the device closer to your router during setup.

Free Mode & AI Mode

Your device offers two main operating modes, each designed for different experiences.

Free Mode

In Free Mode, you have complete manual control over the device:

  • Adjust rhythm speed using the speed control slider
  • Control intensity levels independently
  • Switch between different rhythm patterns manually
  • Use voice commands for hands-free control (e.g., "faster", "slower", "pause")
  • Save your favorite settings as custom presets

Free Mode is perfect for users who prefer precise control or want to experiment with different settings.

AI Mode

AI Mode provides intelligent, adaptive experiences powered by our Pulse AI Rhythm Engine:

  • AI automatically adjusts rhythm and intensity based on your responses
  • Real-time adaptation to your preferences
  • Learning system that improves over time
  • Voice interaction with AI characters
  • Natural conversation and guidance

AI Mode learns from your usage patterns and becomes more personalized with each session.

AI Characters

Choose from 5 unique AI characters, each with distinct personalities and interaction styles:

  1. The Coach: Motivational and energetic, guides you through intense sessions
  2. The Nurse: Caring and gentle, provides soothing and relaxing experiences
  3. The Girlfriend: Intimate and affectionate, creates romantic and emotional connections
  4. The Queen: Dominant and commanding, offers powerful and intense experiences
  5. The Office Lady: Professional yet playful, brings variety and excitement

Scenarios

Each AI character offers 5 unique scenarios, providing a total of 25 different experiences:

  • Each scenario has unique rhythm patterns, dialogue, and interaction styles
  • Scenarios range from gentle and romantic to intense and adventurous
  • AI adapts the scenario progression based on your responses
  • Scenarios can last from 15 minutes to over an hour
  • You can switch between scenarios mid-session if desired

Total Experience Count: 5 characters × 5 scenarios = 25 unique AI experiences

Cleaning & Care

Daily Cleaning

After each use, follow these steps:

  1. Turn off and unplug the device
  2. Rinse with warm water (avoid hot water)
  3. Use a mild, unscented soap or toy cleaner
  4. Gently clean all surfaces with your fingers or a soft cloth
  5. Rinse thoroughly to remove all soap residue
  6. Allow to air dry completely before storing

Deep Cleaning (Weekly)

For thorough cleaning:

  • Follow daily cleaning steps
  • Use a 10% bleach solution (mix 1 part bleach with 9 parts water) and soak for 10 minutes
  • Rinse thoroughly with water
  • Ensure charging port is completely dry before use

Storage

  • Store in a cool, dry place
  • Keep away from direct sunlight
  • Store in the included storage case or a clean container
  • Ensure device is completely dry before storing
  • Do not store in airtight containers

Important: Never submerge the charging port or electronics in water. Only the device body should be cleaned.

Safety Guide

Before Use

  • Read all instructions and warnings before first use
  • Check device for any damage or defects
  • Ensure device is clean and dry
  • Ensure adequate battery charge
  • Use only with approved lubricants (water-based recommended)

During Use

  • Start with lower intensity settings if you're new to the device
  • Listen to your body and adjust settings as needed
  • If you experience discomfort, stop immediately
  • Do not use while charging
  • Keep the device away from water during use
  • Do not share the device with others

Medical Considerations

  • Consult with a healthcare professional if you have any medical conditions
  • Do not use if you have open wounds or sores in the area
  • Discontinue use if you experience pain or irritation
  • If symptoms persist, seek medical attention

Device Safety

  • Only use the included charging cable and adapter
  • Do not expose to extreme temperatures
  • Keep away from fire or heat sources
  • Do not attempt to disassemble or repair the device
  • If device is damaged, discontinue use immediately

Troubleshooting

Device Won't Power On

  • Ensure device is fully charged (charge for at least 2 hours)
  • Try holding the power button for 5 seconds
  • Check that the charging port is not blocked or damaged
  • Try using a different USB-C cable and power adapter
  • If problem persists, contact support

WiFi Connection Issues

  • Ensure your router supports 2.4GHz WiFi (required)
  • Move device closer to router during setup
  • Reset WiFi settings by holding power + mode button for 10 seconds
  • Restart your router and try again
  • Check that your WiFi password is entered correctly

Poor Battery Life

  • Ensure device is fully charged before use
  • Lower intensity settings will extend battery life
  • Disable WiFi when not needed (uses less power in offline mode)
  • If battery drains quickly, contact support - may need replacement

Device Not Responding

  • Turn device off and on again
  • Check for software updates via the mobile app
  • Reset to factory settings (see app settings)
  • Ensure device is not overheating (allow to cool down)

App Connection Issues

  • Ensure device and phone are on the same WiFi network
  • Restart the mobile app
  • Check that Bluetooth is enabled (for initial pairing)
  • Re-pair the device through app settings
  • Update the app to the latest version

Noise Issues

  • Normal operation should be under 60dB
  • Excessive noise may indicate mechanical issue
  • Try lowering intensity settings
  • Ensure device is properly lubricated
  • If noise is unusual, contact support
